Common Questions

Everything You
Need to Know

When should I start working with a book marketing agency? +
Ideally, 3–6 months before your publication date. This allows time for pre-launch awareness, ARC distribution, and building momentum so your launch week begins with energy rather than silence.
Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors? +
Absolutely. We work with authors across all publishing paths — traditional, hybrid, and independent. Our strategies adapt to your distribution model, timeline, and goals regardless of how you publish.
What genres do you specialize in? +
We have deep experience across literary fiction, commercial fiction (romance, thriller, fantasy, sci-fi), narrative nonfiction, memoir, and self-help. Each genre has unique reader communities and marketing dynamics that we know well.
How do you measure the success of a campaign? +
We track KPIs aligned to your goals: pre-order volume, launch week sales rank, review counts, social media engagement, email list growth, and long-tail sales performance. You receive transparent reporting throughout.
Can I hire you for a single service rather than a full package? +
Yes. While our packages offer the best value and most cohesive results, we also offer individual services such as Amazon optimization, social media audits, or ARC campaigns as standalone engagements. Contact us to discuss.
Do you guarantee bestseller status? +
No ethical agency can guarantee a specific rank or list placement. What we guarantee is rigorous strategy, transparent execution, and our full creative effort — we have an exceptional track record and are committed to your success.
